Delivery

About the pieces

Our jewellery is fashion jewellery in plated alloy, not precious metal. Oxidised pieces are silver-plated with an antique finish; gold-toned pieces are gold-plated. The darkening on an oxidised piece is the finish, not tarnish. Keep them away from water and perfume, and wipe with a dry cloth.

Every product page lists the material and the plating. Country of origin is declared on the pack and on our marketplace listings as the law requires.
